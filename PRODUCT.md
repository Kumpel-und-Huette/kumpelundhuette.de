## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary audiences, equally:

- Seniors with Pflegegrad 2 or higher (exceptions by arrangement) who want a shared home instead of living alone or moving into a nursing home. They need to manage the day independently or with light support.
- Relatives deciding whether a parent or partner can move in, and who stay involved after the move.

Job applicants are a secondary audience. The site has a careers page; hiring is not a primary job of the site.

## Product Purpose

Kumpel und Hütte Service runs shared homes (Wohngemeinschaften) where older people live with privacy, company, everyday help, and nursing care. Success is a real home: self-determined, affordable, and not a Pflegeheim, hotel, or anonymous WG.

The published house is in Dortmund-Lütgendortmund, Im Heidewinkel 30, 44267 Dortmund. Copy often speaks of more than one house; only this location is documented.

## Positioning

Kumpel und Hütte provides the home and the nursing care itself. Residents keep their own room and a shared life (kitchen, garden, meals, activities that are offered, not required). The published all-in cost can be covered in full by pension and care-insurance benefits, down to a personal share of 0 €.

Published pages state that the Kumpel und Hütte team provides the nursing care. Future work must not invent an external Pflegedienst or describe the offer as care coordination.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- German is the authoritative language. English pages are a translation, not a separate market.
- Admission target is Pflegegrad 2–5, with exceptions by arrangement, plus willingness to live in a WG.
- Homes are not fully barrier-free. Published adaptations include a floor-level shower, handrails, and considered lighting. Rooms may be furnished by the resident or, by agreement, by the house.
- Support on offer includes household help, meals, laundry, shopping, activating Betreuung, contact with relatives, activities, and in-house nursing care.
- Placeholder facts that must not be treated as real: Umsatzsteuer-ID DE123456789 in the Impressum. Generic social profile URLs are not published. The founding news post contains lorem ipsum tab copy.
- The site is a bilingual Hugo site (theme `hugobricks`), default language German, deployed from the `main` branch.

## Product Principles

- Seniors and relatives are equal audiences. A surface that only informs one of them is incomplete.
- The product is a home with care from Kumpel und Hütte, not a nursing-home institution and not a landlord who only coordinates care.
- Admission rules and the published prices stay exact. Affordability is a claim with numbers attached.
- German copy is the source of truth. English follows it.
- Placeholders and unused proof blocks are not facts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Residents are older people with a care grade who can manage the daytime alone or with light support. The homes are explicitly not fully barrier-free; safety comes from everyday adaptations and staff presence, not from a claimed barrier-free standard. No formal accessibility standard was set for the website.
