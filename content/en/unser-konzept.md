# Together instead of alone: an alternative to a care home

Everyday life at home alone is getting harder, and a care home is not what you want. That is exactly why Kumpel & Hütte exists. With us you live in company, feel safe and shape your own day. Our senior shared home in Dortmund offers space for privacy and for time together, with real quality of life.

## Home starts here

![The kitchen in our senior shared home in Dortmund](/uploads/gallery/Küche.jpg "Our kitchen in Dortmund")

In our senior shared home everyone has their own room. On top of that there are generous spaces that belong to everyone. At breakfast in the living kitchen, in the garden or over a games evening, everyday life is shaped together. What you join in with and when you would rather have some peace is up to you.

## How we differ from a care home

Five things make our senior shared home a real alternative to a care home:

- **A home, not an institution:** You live in a family-like shared home, with your own room and your own furniture.
- **One team for everything:** Nursing care, housekeeping and personal support come from us, from people you know.
- **Your day, your rhythm:** Every shared activity is an invitation, not an obligation.
- **Clear costs:** €970 a month for rent, housekeeping and personal support. Depending on your pension and care grade, your personal share goes down to as little as €0.
- **Family stays close:** Relatives are welcome and closely involved.

You can read what a place costs and what the care insurance covers under [costs and moving in](/en/moving-in/). Why we do all this is explained in [our motivation](/en/motivation/).
