# Our motivation: nobody should be lonely in old age

{{< breadcrumbs >}}

## Why Kumpel & Hütte?

The world keeps moving faster, families live far apart, and in many neighbourhoods people hardly know each other anymore. For many older people that means one thing: they are alone more and more often. At **Kumpel & Hütte** we believe life in old age deserves more. More company, more safety, more dignity. That is why we created an alternative to the classic old-age home: a home that warms, connects and supports, without anyone having to give up who they are.

## The reality for many seniors

Many older people live alone, often in flats that were once meant for a whole family. The children have moved out, a partner may have passed away. What remains is silence. And sometimes loneliness.

On top of that come the questions of everyday life: Who helps with the shopping? Who is there if you fall? Who brings a little light back into the week?

Relatives know the other side. They call every evening, drive over at the weekend and still worry. At some point the question comes up whether it really has to be a care home.

That reality drove us to think differently. "Together instead of alone" should not stay a slogan, but become everyday life.

## The idea

**Kumpel & Hütte** stands for a new way of living in old age: communal, affordable and human. In our senior shared home everyone has their own room to retreat to. But life also happens where community grows: in the living kitchen, the living, reading or games room, or over a cup of tea in the garden.

We support everyday life where it is needed: with the nursing care we provide ourselves, with cooking or on visits to the doctor. At the same time, self-determination is preserved. Nobody is patronised. For us, support does not mean control, it means reliability. That creates a network of mutual support that gives safety without taking away independence. You can see what this looks like day to day in [our concept](/en/our-concept/).

## Encounters create meaning

What keeps confirming our motivation are the encounters, the laughter, the stories. When an afternoon of baking together becomes the highlight of the week, or a walk for two creates a sense of belonging, we know exactly why we do this.

Old age is not a time of standing still, but a time of change. And change needs people who listen, who care and who are simply there. We want to be those people: as companions, as a hut, as a home.

## Affordability and dignity

Good housing in old age must not depend on income. That is why our model is deliberately designed to stay within reach for people with a small pension. The rent is within the limits for basic income support (Grundsicherung) in Dortmund, and from care grade 2 the care insurance can pay for housekeeping and personal support. We work with partners, look for funding options and keep rents fair and transparent. Dignity has no price, but it needs structure. You will find all the numbers under [costs and moving in](/en/moving-in/).

## Shaping the future

Our motivation does not stop at the present. We think ahead: How can we reach even more people? How could the model work in the countryside or in the city? How do we bring young and old together? We believe Kumpel & Hütte can also be a place of learning and of encounter across generations and cultures.

We are working on bringing in digital support without overwhelming anyone, and we are thinking about leisure activities, local partnerships and volunteer programmes. In short: we want to help shape the future of ageing.

## Our drive: humanity

In the end, what matters to us is not a system, but the person. Everyone wants to be seen, heard and needed, and those needs do not end at retirement. We give them room: with open hearts, open arms and an open ear.

**Kumpel & Hütte** is more than a housing concept. It is an attitude, a promise and a home for the next chapter of life. If you feel it is time for this step, for yourself or for your parents, [call us or write to us](/en/contact/).
