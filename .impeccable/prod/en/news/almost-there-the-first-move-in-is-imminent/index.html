Almost there — the first move-in is imminent

Only a few days left until the first resident moves into our senior shared home! Final preparations are already in full swing: the photo shows our carpenter “Conny” fitting handrails — small details that make a big difference in everyday life.

We are thrilled about this special moment and about our house finally being filled with life. The first shared cup of coffee, the first laughter at the table — it is all just around the corner.

An exciting new chapter begins — and we can hardly wait to experience it together.

Learn more about your new home!

Kumpel und Hütte offers communal living for seniors — safe, affordable and with heart. In our caring shared homes, self-determination, community and joy of life take centre stage.
