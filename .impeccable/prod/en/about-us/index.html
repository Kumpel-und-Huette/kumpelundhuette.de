About us

Behind Kumpel und Hütte is a dedicated team of people who share one vision: making life in older age more beautiful, safer and more communal. We come from different fields — opera, ballet, management and investment — but what connects us is the wish to create meaningful housing concepts with genuine humanity. With experience, openness and a real ear for individual needs, we accompany our residents in everyday life — not as mere service providers, but as companions and support.

  • Robin und Julianne Grunwald

    Managing directors and founders of Kumpel & Hütte

    Kumpel & Hütte is our heartfelt project! With the vision of countering growing loneliness especially in older age and enabling people who need support to live with dignity, self-determination and community. What began as a personal idea is growing into a housing concept with a future. Familial, affordable and human. We believe nobody has to stay alone in older age — if we do things differently.

    robin@kumpelundhuette.de

  • Guilia

    Carer and support worker

    For me, working in the shared home is much more than a job. It is a place where I can spend time with people as equals — with all their stories, quirks and very individual needs. I am glad to help with the things that have become harder in everyday life. But what matters even more to me: I am simply there. With open ears, an honest laugh and the calm that is needed — including for the quiet moments. In the shared home, real togetherness grows. People eat together, laugh, sometimes stay silent — but never forget that every person counts. I am proud to be part of it. Because the work with the residents touches, moves and gives me back just as much as I put in.

Want to join us?

Would you like to make life in older age more human and more communal? At Kumpel und Hütte we are always looking for dedicated, warm-hearted people who share our vision. Whether from care, social work, design — or simply with a lot of heart: there is a place for you with us.

Let’s make a difference together.
