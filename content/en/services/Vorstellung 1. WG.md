On 4 June 2025 I had the chance to present our first senior shared home at the round table of the Seniorenbüro Lütgendortmund.

It was a special moment for me, because it became clear: communal living in old age is more than a trend. It is a real chance for quality of life, self-determination and togetherness.

The positive response showed us that our idea has struck a chord. And it encourages us to keep going: to create senior-friendly ways of living that bring together heart, mind and everyday practice.

We are excited about the next steps and look forward to taking them together with strong partners and wonderful people.
