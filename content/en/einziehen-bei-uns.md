# Moving into our senior shared home: costs, care insurance and steps

{{< breadcrumbs >}}

Maybe you have noticed that living alone at home is no longer working well. Maybe you are worried about your mother or father. And maybe you are wondering whether you can afford a place at all. Here you will find everything you need to decide: who can move in, what a place in our senior shared home in Dortmund costs, what the care insurance covers and how moving in works, step by step.

## Your life in our senior shared home

Together instead of alone. Here is what to expect in [our house in Dortmund](/en/our-home/dortmund-lütgendortmund/):

- **Privacy and community:** Your own room, plus a generous living kitchen, two modern shower rooms, a garden and a shared living room.
- **Everyday life with heart:** Shared meals, activities, celebrations and small outings. Everything is possible, nothing is mandatory.
- **Help where you need it:** Our team supports you with the housework, everyday life and nursing care. We provide the nursing care ourselves.
- **Safety:** An emergency call system around the clock by arrangement, Wi-Fi and senior-friendly furnishings.
- **Clear costs:** One fixed amount with no hidden fees. Depending on your pension and care grade, your personal share can go down to €0.

## Who can move in

Our senior shared home suits you if you

- have **care grade 2 or higher** (Pflegegrad 2; exceptions are possible by arrangement),
- manage during the day on your own or with light support,
- are open to living in a shared home and
- hold a valid assessment from the care insurance if you have a care grade.

Couples are welcome, and a pet is also possible by arrangement. You can see what life with us feels like under [senior living in Dortmund](/en/our-home/). How we differ from a care home is explained in [our concept](/en/our-concept/).

## What a place in the senior shared home costs

You pay rent, housekeeping and personal support as one amount. This is how it adds up:

|Item|Included services|Price|Support|Own contribution|
|--|--|--|--|--|
| Rent | Room rent, utilities, electricity, heating, internet, broadcasting fee | €570 | Possible depending on pension | **€0–570** |
| Household | Weekly cleaning of all rooms, weekly shopping, weekly laundry service, daily breakfast, daily hot meal | €200 | Care fund (from care grade 2) | **€0–200** |
| Personal support | Activating support, general and individual organisation, conversations, contact with relatives, and more | €200 | Care fund (from care grade 2) | **€0–200** |
| **Total** | Everything included | €970 | Fully covered by arrangement and care grade | **€0–970** |

### What the care insurance and the social welfare office can cover

From care grade 2, two benefits from the care insurance (Pflegekasse) are the main options for housekeeping and personal support: the **residential group supplement under § 38a SGB XI** (Wohngruppenzuschlag) and the **relief amount** (Entlastungsbetrag). The rent of €570 is within the limits for **basic income support (Grundsicherung) in Dortmund**. If your pension is not enough, we check with you whether the social welfare office (Sozialamt) can step in. We work out together what applies in your case before you move in. You do not have to deal with the paperwork alone.

## Process in 6 steps

1. **Get in touch:** Call or write to us. We listen and answer your first questions.
2. **Viewing:** Take your time to look around the house, ideally together with your family.
3. **Getting to know each other:** Meet the people who already live here, for example over coffee and cake. People who are going to live together should know each other first.
4. **Application and documents:** Submit the registration form and medical certificates.
5. **Clarify costs:** Together we sort out the care insurance, the social welfare office and any additional services.
6. **Move-in and welcome week:** Arrive, settle in, get to know your new housemates better and start your new everyday life.

We are happy to help with every step.

## Required documents

- Fully completed **registration form**
- Medical **fitness-to-live certificate**
- **Care grade notice** (if available)
- **Insurance card** and medication list
- **Powers of attorney** or care directives (optional)
- **Contact details** of relatives or legal guardians

Something missing? No problem. We will tell you exactly what we need.
