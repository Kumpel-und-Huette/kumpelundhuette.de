Kumpel & Hütte Service is more than a place to live – it is a real home. Not a care home. Not a hotel. Not an anonymous shared flat. Instead, a familiar community for people who want to grow old together rather than alone – with dignity, humour and practical everyday support.

What we offer

At Kumpel und Hütte we create a home for people who want to keep living independently despite needing care — in a safe, caring community. Our concept is for people with care grade 2 or higher who need support in daily life but do not want a traditional nursing home.

Our promise

  • Communal living in a warm atmosphere
  • Practical everyday support and individual help
  • Encouraging self-determination, activity and joy of life
  • Sustainable and affordable housing
  • Trusting, empathetic support from our qualified team

What we stand for

At Kumpel und Hütte we want people who need care to live a fulfilling, self-determined life. Our values shape everyday life in our shared homes and create an environment where people feel welcome and well looked after.

  • Trust — Residents and families can rely on us: transparent, responsible and dependable.

  • Warmth — Our houses are real homes: friendly, familiar and shaped by care.

  • Community — We foster connections between residents, families, staff and carers.

  • Dignity — Every resident is treated with respect, self-determination and individual attention.

  • Affordability — Our model is fairly priced, eases the load on residents and relatives, and is comprehensively supported by care benefits.

Our homes at a glance

Kumpel und Hütte gives you everything that makes for a safe, self-determined and sheltered life despite needing care. Our house communities are thoughtfully designed and combine comfort with genuine closeness — for people who do not want to be alone in everyday life.

  • Spaces for meeting and retreat

    With us, privacy and community go hand in hand. Every home offers room for personal comfort and spaces to come together: a living kitchen, a cosy living room, a games or reading corner and a green garden invite conversation. You can find out what amenities await you in our offers.

  • Safe and well-thought-out furnishings

    Our flats are not fully barrier-free, but they are practical for everyday life and carefully fitted out. Walk-in showers, handrails and well-planned lighting support orientation, comfort and safety. So self-determined living remains possible — with furnishings that support without constraining.

  • Individually furnished rooms

    Every room can be set up to your own wishes with familiar personal belongings, or furnished by us by arrangement. That creates a personal retreat that truly feels like home.

  • Modern communication and technology

    Wi-Fi, home emergency call systems by arrangement and solid digital basics provide connection and safety. Many solutions can be tailored individually — for more comfort in everyday life.

  • Nursing and everyday help from our team

    Our own team provides household help, accompaniment, support, and the nursing care.

  • Community, culture and joy of life

    Whether shared meals, outings, reading circles or music: Everyday life stays lively — everyone can join in, nobody has to.

Questions and answers

Living at Kumpel und Hütte

Who is Kumpel und Hütte for?
Our shared homes are for people with care grade 2 or higher who want to live safely, affordably and in company — with as much support as needed and as much self-determination as possible.
How much independence do residents keep?
Self-determination is one of our core values. Our residents shape their everyday life themselves and decide where and when they want support.
What activities are offered?
Throughout the year we organise small celebrations and outings: barbecue evenings, birthday parties, cinema and theatre visits as well as cultural events — for more joy of life and community.

Costs and services

Is living at Kumpel und Hütte affordable?
Yes, we place great importance on affordable housing. Our goal is to make high-quality, safe living accessible to as many people as possible.
What services are included?
Alongside the room, we ourselves provide household help, support and nursing care. Activities and accompaniment are there when they are needed.
Are there long-term housing options?
Yes, we offer stable, long-term housing solutions — Kumpel und Hütte is not a short-term offer, but a real home.

Safety and trust

How is residents’ safety ensured?
Our flats are designed so that residents feel safe and sheltered. Our team provides supportive presence and looks after a trusting community.
Can family members get involved?
Very much so. We actively encourage exchange and involvement of relatives and work closely with families to ensure the best possible care.
How transparent is the care?
Transparency and trust are a given for us. Relatives and residents always receive clear information about all services and processes.
