# Senior living in Dortmund

Living alone in your flat is getting hard, and a care home does not feel like you? Then come and see our house. People live here in their own room, with real company, and with support from our team when they need it. That support is optional.

Senior living should feel like home. With us, that means:

- your own room, furnished with your own things,
- shared meals in the living kitchen, plus a living room and garden for everyone,
- support you need, optionally, from our team or from cooperation partners.

Our pilot shared home “Glück auf” is a senior shared home for people with care grade 2 or higher. Exceptions are possible by arrangement.

How moving in works is explained under [costs and moving in](/en/moving-in/), and how we differ from a care home in [our concept](/en/our-concept/). The quickest way to find out whether a room is available is to call us on [+49 160 90889269](tel:+4916090889269).
