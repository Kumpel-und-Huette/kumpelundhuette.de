# Elderly care jobs in Dortmund
{{< breadcrumbs >}}

Are you looking for a job where, at the end of the day, you know why you do it? At Kumpel & Hütte you help older people have a real home in our [senior shared home in Dortmund](/en/). You cook together, you listen, you help around the house and you are there when someone needs you.

We are a small, warm-hearted team working on a big vision. Experience in housekeeping or personal support is nice, but not a requirement. Empathy, patience and reliability matter more to us. We work out together which job suits you.

## Why this work matters

Many older people are lonely, even in the middle of a city. And many families reach a point where they no longer know how to fit care, work and everyday life together. With us, you bring company, structure and a laugh into the day. Why this matters so much to us is explained in [our motivation](/en/motivation/). You can meet the people you would work with under [who we are](/en/about-us/).
