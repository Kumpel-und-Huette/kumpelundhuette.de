On 6 May 2025 we signed the partnership agreement for our Kumpel & Hütte UG (haftungsbeschränkt) — a moment we celebrate with pride and joy.

“Kumpel & Hütte” is more than just a name for us. It stands for solidarity, honest work, friendship and a place where ideas are born and become reality. These are exactly the values we want to carry into our company.

With the founding we have now laid the foundation to pursue our projects with full strength. For us that means: roll up our sleeves, get stuck in together and build something new with heart and passion.

We look forward to what comes next — and to sharing this journey with you.
