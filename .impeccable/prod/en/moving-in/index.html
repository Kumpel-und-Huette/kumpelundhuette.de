Welcome to Kumpel und Hütte!

Here you can learn what living in our communal homes looks like, what requirements apply and how the move-in process works step by step.

Your life in the community

Together instead of alone — with us you can expect

  • Privacy & community: Your own comfortable room plus a generous living kitchen, modern bathroom, garden or balcony and shared living space.
  • Everyday life with heart: Shared meals, activities, celebrations and cultural outings — everything is possible, nothing is mandatory.
  • Individual support: Our team helps where it is needed — from household help through to the nursing care we provide ourselves.
  • Safety & technology: 24/7 emergency call system by arrangement, Wi-Fi and senior-friendly furnishings for a carefree life.
  • Transparent costs: Clear pricing with no hidden fees; financing support through care and social funds may be possible.

Requirements & admission

To move in with us, you should

  • have care grade 2 or higher (exceptions possible by arrangement),
  • manage during the day independently or with light support,
  • be open to living in a shared home,
  • hold a valid care-fund classification when a care grade is in place,

Process in 5 steps

  1. Get in touch — By phone or form.
  2. Viewing — Get to know the house, clarify questions.
  3. Application & documents — Submit registration form and certificates.
  4. Cost clarification — Align care fund / social office + additional services.
  5. Move-in & welcome week — Arrive, get to know each other, start the new everyday life.

We are happy to help with every step.

Required documents

  • Fully completed registration form
  • Medical fitness-to-live certificate
  • Care grade notice (if available)
  • Insurance card & medication list
  • Powers of attorney / care directives (optional)
  • Contact details of relatives or legal guardians

Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

Living at Kumpel und Hütte

Who is Kumpel und Hütte for?
Our shared homes are for people with care grade 2 or higher who want to live safely, affordably and in company — with as much support as needed and as much self-determination as possible.
How much independence do residents keep?
Self-determination is one of our core values. Our residents shape their everyday life themselves and decide where and when they want support.
What activities are offered?
Throughout the year we organise small celebrations and outings: barbecue evenings, birthday parties, cinema and theatre visits as well as cultural events — for more joy of life and community.

Costs and services

Is living at Kumpel und Hütte affordable?
Yes, we place great importance on affordable housing. Our goal is to make high-quality, safe living accessible to as many people as possible.
What services are included?
Alongside the room, we ourselves provide household help, support and nursing care. Activities and accompaniment are there when they are needed.
Are there long-term housing options?
Yes, we offer stable, long-term housing solutions — Kumpel und Hütte is not a short-term offer, but a real home.

Safety and trust

How is residents’ safety ensured?
Our flats are designed so that residents feel safe and sheltered. Our team provides supportive presence and looks after a trusting community.
Can family members get involved?
Very much so. We actively encourage exchange and involvement of relatives and work closely with families to ensure the best possible care.
How transparent is the care?
Transparency and trust are a given for us. Relatives and residents always receive clear information about all services and processes.
