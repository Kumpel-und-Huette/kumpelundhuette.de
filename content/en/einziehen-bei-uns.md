# Moving into our homes: costs, care insurance and steps

{{< breadcrumbs >}}

Maybe you have noticed that living alone at home is no longer working well. Maybe you are worried about your mother or father. And maybe you are wondering whether you can afford a place at all. Here you will find everything you need to decide: who can move in, what a place in our senior shared home in Dortmund costs, what the care insurance covers and how moving in works, step by step.

## Your life in our shared home with care

Together instead of alone. Here is what to expect, for example, in our [shared home “Glück auf” in Dortmund](/en/our-home/dortmund-lütgendortmund/):

- **Privacy and community:** Your own room, plus a generous living kitchen, two modern shower rooms, a garden and a shared living room.
- **Everyday life with heart:** Shared meals, activities, celebrations and small outings. Everything is possible, nothing is mandatory.
- **Help where you need it:** Our team supports you with the housework, everyday life and care coordination. Our cooperation partners provide the nursing care, in agreement with you and with us.
- **Safety:** An emergency call system around the clock by arrangement, Wi-Fi and senior-friendly furnishings.
- **Clear costs:** Only the all-in rent of €570 is mandatory. Housekeeping and personal support are an optional service contract.

## Who can move in

Our shared home “Glück auf” suits you if you

- have **care grade 2 or higher** (Pflegegrad 2; exceptions are possible by arrangement),
- manage during the day on your own or with light support,
- are open to living in a shared home.

Couples are welcome, and a pet is also possible by arrangement. In the end, our residents decide on new housemates and on pets. You can see what life with us feels like under [senior living in Dortmund](/en/our-home/). How we differ from a care home, for example, is explained in [our concept](/en/our-concept/).

## What a place with us costs

You will find the price on the page for that accommodation.

**Rent, the only mandatory amount:** €570 all-inclusive for electricity, gas, cold utilities, internet and the TV licence if applicable. If needed, the social welfare office can cover the rent.

**Optional service contract** for housekeeping and personal support: prices depend on what you need, hourly billing is possible, and they follow state guidelines. More information at [heykumpel.com](https://www.heykumpel.com). With a care grade, billing through the care insurance (Pflegekasse) is possible.

### What the care insurance and the social welfare office can cover

From care grade 2, care-insurance benefits can cover everyday support: the **residential group supplement under § 38a SGB XI** (Wohngruppenzuschlag) from 3 people, and the **relief amount** (Entlastungsbetrag). In addition, 40% of in-kind care benefits (Pflegesachleistungen) can be converted, and the respite-care budget (Verhinderungspflege) of €3,539 can be used for our services. From 2027 the new PNOG is due to take effect. The budgets will then change. We will say more if the law comes into force in that form. The rent of €570 is within the limits for **basic income support (Grundsicherung) in Dortmund**. If your pension is not enough, we check with you whether the social welfare office (Sozialamt) can step in. We work out together what applies in your case before you move in. You do not have to deal with the paperwork alone.

### Required documents

After a successful viewing, and if you intend to move in:

- Fully completed **registration form**. You receive it on request via our [contact form](/en/contact/) or by phone.
- **Care grade notice** (if available)
- **Insurance card** and medication list
- **Powers of attorney** or care directives (optional)
- **Contact details** of relatives or legal guardians

Something missing? No problem. We will tell you exactly what we need.

## Process in 6 steps

1. **Get in touch:** Call or write to us. We listen and answer your first questions.
2. **Viewing:** Take your time to look around the house, ideally together with your family.
3. **Getting to know each other:** Meet the people who already live here, for example over coffee and cake. People who are going to live together should know each other first.
4. **Application and documents:** Submit the registration form and the other documents.
5. **Clarify costs:** Together we sort out the care insurance, the social welfare office and any additional services.
6. **Move-in and welcome week:** Arrive, settle in, get to know your new housemates better and start your new everyday life.

We are happy to help with every step.
