# Together instead of alone: an alternative to a care home

Everyday life at home alone is getting harder, and you do not want to move into a care home. That is exactly why Kumpel & Hütte exists. With us you live in company, feel safe and shape your own day. Our “Glück auf” shared home in Dortmund offers space for privacy and for time together, with real quality of life.

## Home starts here

![The kitchen in our senior shared home in Dortmund](/uploads/gallery/Küche.jpg "Our kitchen in Dortmund")

## How we differ from a care home

Five things make our senior shared home a real alternative to a care home:

- **A home, not an institution:** You live in a family-like shared home, with your own room and your own furniture.
- **Right of the house:** With us, the shared home holds the right of the house. You decide who provides care and help, how much, how often and for how long. You also decide who becomes a new housemate. We step in only if that is what you want.
- **Your day, your rhythm:** Every shared activity is an invitation, not an obligation.
- **Clear costs:** Only the all-in rent is mandatory. Housekeeping and personal support are optional. If needed, the social welfare office can cover the rent, and the care insurance can cover nursing care and everyday support. No hidden costs.
- **Family stays close:** Relatives are welcome and closely involved.

You can read what a place costs and what the care insurance covers under [costs and moving in](/en/moving-in/). Why we do all this is explained in [our motivation](/en/motivation/).
