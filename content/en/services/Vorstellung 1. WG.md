On 4 June 2025 I had the chance to present our first senior shared home at the round table of the Seniorenbüro Lütgendortmund.

It was a special moment for me, because it became clear: communal living in older age is more than a trend — it is a real opportunity for quality of life, self-determination and togetherness.

The positive response showed us that our idea meets the spirit of the times. And it encourages us to keep going: creating senior-friendly forms of living that connect heart, mind and everyday practice.

We are excited about the next steps — and look forward to shaping them together with strong partners and wonderful people.
